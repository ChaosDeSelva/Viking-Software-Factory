# Code of conduct

We want Sindri to be a respectful, harassment free project for everyone.

* Be kind and assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.
* Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
* Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may decline further participation from those who repeatedly do.

Report conduct concerns to the reporting process in SECURITY.md Reports are handled privately.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
