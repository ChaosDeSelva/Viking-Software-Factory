# Code of conduct

We want Mimir to be a place where people can do good technical work without being harassed or
belittled.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
* Give and accept feedback on code and design plainly and kindly.
* Keep discussion on topic and credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
* Publishing someone's private information.
* Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to every project space: the repository, reviews, and any discussion channel. Maintainers
may edit, reject or remove contributions and may restrict participation for conduct that breaks
these rules. Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Jacob Lashley
(the reporting process in SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
