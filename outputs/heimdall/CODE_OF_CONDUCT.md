# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss technical choices with evidence, welcome questions and differing experience levels, and give credit for others' work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the repository's contact channel. Handle reports confidentially and disclose only what is needed to resolve them fairly.
